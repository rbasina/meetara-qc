# Mini Project A: Noise Impact Comparison

## Goal
Compare the same circuit under ideal simulation and a basic noisy simulation, then summarize quality impact.

## Deliverables
1. Notebook with experiment steps.
2. Table of counts and deviations.
3. One-page interpretation note.

## Required sections
1. Circuit definition.
2. Expected ideal distribution.
3. Noisy run setup.
4. Results comparison.
5. Interpretation and limitations.

## Constraints
1. Keep total runs small to control compute.
2. Use one or two circuits only.
3. Use fixed random seed where possible.
