# Syllabus: Quantum Evaluation Zero to Hero

## Program format
1. Foundation pre-chapter plus 12 core chapters.
2. Three-tier progression: beginner, intermediate, advanced.
3. Every chapter includes concept, practical, evaluation checkpoint, and quiz.

## Chapter-Notebook Sync
1. Beginner: notebooks 01 to 04
2. Intermediate: notebooks 05 to 08
3. Advanced: notebooks 09 to 12
4. Detailed mapping is maintained in meeTARA-QC.md

Use the chapters for conceptual learning first, then use the matching notebook for hands-on practice.

## Foundation pre-chapter
0. Classical vs quantum computing, history, and AI relevance.

## Tier 1: Beginner (Chapters 1-4)
1. Qubits and intuition.
2. Gates and circuits.
3. Measurement and probabilities.
4. Simulator basics and first benchmark.

Tier-1 outcome:
Learners can run controlled simulator experiments and judge if observed outcomes are statistically consistent with expectations.

## Tier 2: Intermediate (Chapters 5-8)
5. Fidelity and distance-based quality metrics.
6. Noise models and error interpretation.
7. Algorithm evaluation (Grover and basic variational forms).
8. Simulator vs hardware comparison design.

Tier-2 outcome:
Learners can evaluate quality degradation from noise and produce fair comparisons across execution environments.

## Tier 3: Advanced (Chapters 9-12)
9. Resource metrics (depth, width, shots, runtime).
10. Reproducible benchmark methodology.
11. VQE and QAOA evaluation strategy.
12. Capstone benchmarking study and technical report.

Tier-3 outcome:
Learners can produce expert-grade benchmark reports with methodology, significance analysis, and reproducibility artifacts.

## Assessment model
1. Chapter quizzes: 10 questions each.
2. Mini Project A: noise impact benchmark.
3. Mini Project B: reproducible simulator-vs-hardware report.
4. Capstone: end-to-end algorithm evaluation report.

## Completion criteria
1. Beginner quizzes at 70 percent or higher.
2. Both mini projects submitted with passing rubric score.
3. Capstone includes reproducibility section and limitation analysis.

## Learner Support Layer
1. Each chapter starts with Plain-English First.
2. Each chapter includes Quick Terms in this Chapter.
3. Each notebook includes a Cell-by-cell Guide section.
4. Real-world scenarios are maintained in chapters/REAL_WORLD_EXAMPLES_PLAYBOOK.md.
