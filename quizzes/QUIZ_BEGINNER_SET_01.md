# Quiz: Beginner Set 01 (Chapters 1-4)

## Questions
1. What is the key difference between a classical bit and a qubit?
2. What does the H gate generally do to |0>?
3. Why are repeated shots needed in quantum experiments?
4. In a Bell state setup, which output pairs are expected most often?
5. What is a good first environment for benchmark development: simulator or hardware?
6. If observed output is far from expected output, name two possible causes.
7. What does measurement collapse refer to?
8. Why do higher shot counts improve estimate stability?
9. What is a deterministic circuit example from beginner chapters?
10. What should be documented for each benchmark run?

## Answer key (short)
1. Qubit can represent superposition; classical bit is 0 or 1.
2. Creates equal superposition from |0>.
3. To estimate probability distribution reliably.
4. 00 and 11.
5. Simulator.
6. Circuit bug, sampling variance, or noise.
7. State maps to classical outcome.
8. Lower variance in frequency estimates.
9. X flip with measurement.
10. Expected, observed, deviation, pass or investigate.
